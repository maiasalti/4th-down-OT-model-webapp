How it works

For each choice, the engine builds the game state that follows (converted, failed, kick made, kick missed, punted) and scores it with a win-probability model. The best choice is the one with the highest expected win probability.

Rules, method, and limits
  • Post-2022 overtime: both teams get the ball; after that, the next score wins.
  • Missed field goals give the opponent the ball at the spot of the kick, or their 20 if that is closer to their goal.
  • The field-goal model is constrained so a longer kick or stronger wind never helps, and is tapered past 57 yards where data is thin.
  • The win-probability model is trained on regulation plays; overtime states are mapped onto regulation equivalents. It changes little once a team is pinned inside its own 30.
  • Punt quality uses team-level, not punter-level, stats.
