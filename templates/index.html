How the call is made

Each choice leads to a few outcomes: convert or get stopped, make or miss, a punt that lands somewhere. The engine estimates how likely each outcome is, then how likely you are to win from where it leaves the game. The call is the choice with the best expected chance to win.

Overtime is valued drive by drive under the 2025 rules: both teams get the ball, then the next score wins. That is why field position matters so much. Give the ball back at your own 10 and the other team only needs a field goal.
